# E-Commerce_Performance_Dashboard_Power-BI

🛒 E-Commerce Dashboard
This dashboard evaluates business performance, tracking sales growth, profitability, and customer transaction habits.
Overall Performance: Highlights key business figures including Total Quantity sold (27K), Total Sales (12.78M), and Total Profit (2.22M).  
Product Insights: Analyzes the sum of profit and sales across major sectors like Home, Electronics, Beauty, and Clothing.  
Payment Preferences: Visualizes the sum of profit by payment modes, tracking transactions via Cash on Delivery, Credit Card, Debit Card, and UPI.
